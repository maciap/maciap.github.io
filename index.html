I am a postdoctoral researcher at Scuola Normale Superiore in Pisa, in the Data Science and Human-centered AI group and the KDD Lab led by Prof. Fosca Giannotti. My current research focuses on explainable and trustworthy AI, the evaluation and explanation of large language model behavior, and applications of AI to scientific discovery. I am a work package and task leader in the ERC Proof of Concept project ILLUME-4-Science.

Previously, I was a postdoctoral researcher at Aalto University with Prof. Heikki Mannila, and I received my D.Sc. (Tech.) in Computer Science from Aalto University in 2024, advised by Prof. Aristides Gionis. Before that, I studied statistics and data science at the University of Bologna, the University of Glasgow, and Sapienza University of Rome, and did research at ISI Foundation in Turin.

My work has appeared at venues including NeurIPS, KDD, SIGMOD, IJCAI, ICDE, ICDM and ECML PKDD, and in journals such as Data Mining and Knowledge Discovery and ACM Transactions on Knowledge Discovery from Data.

Research

Explainable & trustworthy AI

Explaining and evaluating the behavior of large language models, including behavioral shifts, and tools for mechanistic interpretability and explainability of transformers.

AI for science

Explainable AI for biomedical applications, such as cancer drug response prediction and risk factor identification from high-dimensional data.

Interpretable machine learning

Concise, interpretable rule-based models and the exploration of the Rashomon set of equally accurate models.

Efficient algorithms for data

Matrix decompositions, low-rank approximation and clustering, efficient probabilistic inference, path optimization, and temporal network analysis.
